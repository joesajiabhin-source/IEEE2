01 Event details // about VYORA

A platform for bold ideas

VYORA '26 is a national-level AI-focused technical and community event by IEEE Student Branch and the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society Chapter at Vimal Jyothi Engineering College, Kannur. It brings students, creators and problem-solvers together to explore, learn and build.

Our Vision

To turn classroom theory into shipped work. Every participant leaves with a repository, a demo, and a mentor they can keep talking to — not just a certificate.

  • Project-first pedagogy over lecture-first delivery.
  • Mentors drawn from industry and senior IEEE volunteers.
  • Open, documented outputs that outlive the event.

Summer School Objectives

  • Give students hands-on exposure to current engineering toolchains.
  • Bridge the gap between academic syllabus and industry practice.
  • Build a peer network across colleges in the Kerala Section.
  • Encourage IEEE membership through demonstrated value.
  • Seed year-round project teams from the cohort.

Who should attend

Second- and third-year engineering students across CSE, ECE, EEE and allied branches. Basic programming familiarity is enough — tracks ramp from fundamentals.

What you take home

A working project, a documented repository, IEEE-issued participation credentials, and access to the alumni mentor channel.

Format

Two full days on campus. Mornings are guided labs, afternoons are build blocks, and each day closes with a review panel and demos.

02 Time schedule // plan your day

Event journey

Two days of ideas, learning, AI and shared experiences. Select a day to inspect the program.

08:30 — 09:30

Registration & Kit Handover

Check-in at the academic block, badge and kit collection, track room allocation.

09:30 — 10:15

Inauguration & Keynote

Welcome address by the branch counsellor, followed by the opening industry keynote.

10:30 — 13:00

Track Lab — Session I

Fundamentals and environment setup inside your chosen track. Everything installed and running before lunch.

14:00 — 16:30

Track Lab — Session II

Guided build. Mentors circulate; checkpoint commits at the end of each hour.

16:45 — 18:00

Review Panel & Networking

Cross-track review of day-one progress, followed by an open networking hour.

09:00 — 09:30

Standup & Day-Two Brief

Each cohort states its build target for the day in two minutes.

09:30 — 12:30

Track Lab — Session III

The main build block. Integration, debugging, and a mid-block mentor review.

13:30 — 15:00

Challenge Round

Track-specific challenge — CTF, line-follow race, deployment sprint or model bake-off.

15:15 — 16:30

Project Showcase

Every team demos to the full cohort. Five minutes each, live only — no slides.

16:30 — 17:30

Valediction & Certificates

Awards, IEEE membership drive, and certificate distribution.
